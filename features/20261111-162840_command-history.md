## Blocking decisions

None. Settled 2026-11-11: decisions 1, 3 and 4 as proposed (Bill: "Yes, start building"); 2 as below.

1. **What counts as a command.** Proposal: every request that reaches the assistant (typed or spoken, from any device), with its reply and tool calls. Not saved: wake words with no speech after them, and requests dropped as no speech or as Whisper's prompt echo.
2. **How long to keep it.** Bill, 2026-11-11: "lets just save everythign and we'll see how fast the databae grows, no archive". This replaces his earlier "7 days and an archival rotation" (built, then taken out the same day). Everything stays in the database. The `history` tool still looks back only 7 days (`history.search_days`; Bill: "Only 7 days"), and `assistant history search` reads all of it.
3. **Who can read it.** Proposal: the `history` tool is for the fast model on any household device (the house shares one history, like timers today), and the household dashboard gets no history view in this feature. History is never sent to cloud agents (Claude): their `share_context` stays "task and room only". A local agent (Hermes) doesn't get it either unless a later feature adds it.
4. **What a lookup returns.** Proposal: at most 10 matching requests, newest first, each as time, room, what was said and the reply. The model then answers from those in one or two spoken sentences.

## Notes

- Nothing stores commands today. They show up only in the service log (`assistant-error.log`, rotated and gzipped by the daily job), and the conversation keeps only recent turns. Since 2026-11-11 it also drops out-of-date answers ([forget-out-of-date-answers](20261111-154338_forget-out-of-date-answers.md)).
- The architecture additions (§8, §11) were approved with the go-ahead (Bill: "Yes, start building").
- Privacy: this keeps a record of everything said to the assistant in the house, and the nightly backup carries it.

## Evidence

Built 2026-11-11:

- `history` table (migration `0009_history.sql`) and `victoria_voice_assistant/history.py`: the store, the per-session recorder and the tool's search.
- Saving: a request is saved when it begins (`TurnPreprocessor._handled`, shared by chat, browser voice and satellites), each tool call as it runs (`ToolRuntime`), direct replies in `_reply`, and the model's or a tool's spoken replies by `HistoryTap`, placed after the turn tracker in all three pipelines. The room comes from the device registry (`rooms.room_of`). A reply more than 120 s after its request isn't added to it.
- The `history` tool (`kind: builtin`, `service: history`) in both `tools.yaml` files, with `words`, `room` and `when` (`last_hour`, `today`, `this_morning`, `this_afternoon`, `this_evening`, `last_night`, `yesterday`, `this_week`). It searches no further back than `keep_days`, leaves out its own request, and returns at most 10, newest first.
- `history: search_days: 7` in both `tools.yaml` files (`HistoryConfig`).
- ~~Nightly move to monthly archive files, carried by the backup~~: built, then removed at Bill's word (no archive). Everything stays in the database; the daily job and the backup are as before.
- `assistant history search [WORDS] [--since DATE] [--room ROOM]` searches everything; `assistant history stats` gives the count, the oldest request and the space used.
- Agents: `assistant config check` refuses the history tool in any agent's `tools`.
- Docs: architecture v2.13 (§8 "Built-in command history", §11), new `docs/history.md`.
